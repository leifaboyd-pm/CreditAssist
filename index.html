Privacy The report is sent to Anthropic for vision-based extraction, then discarded. Extracted data is stored only in your browser's local storage on this device. Nothing is saved to a Partners Mortgage server. Click Reset & Clear to wipe.

What it does not do It does not predict exact FICO scores, advise on disputes, or replace a credit counselor.
